You classify one risk factor from Item 1A (Risk Factors) of an SEC 10-K or 10-Q filing.
A risk factor is a lead sentence (s1, its title) and the sentences under it, in order.
Choose themes only from the list at the end, by theme_id, and follow the boundary rules in
their definitions.

primary_theme: the one theme the risk factor as a whole is about. Judge the risk itself,
mostly what the lead sentence says can go wrong, not every cause, example or consequence it
mentions. Almost every risk factor says it could harm results, reputation or the stock price;
that alone is not a theme. Answer "unknown" only when no theme fits at all.

secondary_themes: up to two other themes that some sentences discuss as a distinct risk in
their own right, each with the ids of the sentences that discuss it. Leave the list empty
when the other sentences only explain, illustrate or give consequences of the primary theme.
Never repeat the primary theme, and use only the sentence ids given.

### Retrieval — Alison

- [ ] **Abstention, per arm.** The arms score on different scales — the graph arm
  passes through Neo4j's full-text relevance, which is unbounded and moves with the
  corpus; the structured arm uses our own formula, capped near 1.0, where zero means
  the fact does not answer. One shared number cannot be right for both. Done when:
  each arm has its own floor and tie rule, and each number records what it was
  fitted on.
- [ ] **Future-period questions abstain on coverage, not on score.** Five of the
  twelve round 1 failures ask about a quarter nobody has filed yet, so the arm
  retrieves strong, relevant evidence for the wrong period and no floor will ever
  catch it. The check is a lookup: compare the period the question asks for against
  the latest `reporting_period_end` we hold **for that company** — fiscal calendars
  differ, and one of these filings is a 53-week year. Done when: the five abstain
  with a reason, and none of them needs a threshold to do it.
- [ ] Period-aware ranking first, because the check above rests on it. The structured
  ranker never reads `period_start` or `period_end`, so a nine-month figure ties with
  the three-month one and nothing can tell a wrong-period fact from a right one.
  Done when: two facts differing only in period no longer score the same.
- [ ] **Text2Cypher — shadow Adeline, own the guard and the log.** Not a build item
  this week; the guard has nothing to sit on until step 1 lands. Done when: the two
  interface questions in [text2cypher-scope.md](../text2cypher-scope.md) are
  answered — does the guard refuse by raising or by returning a reason, and what one
  line does the log write per call.
- [ ] **E7, retriever comparison.** From the W8 board. Done when: the two arms are
